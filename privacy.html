TapRightHere Privacy Policy

TapRightHere has no accounts and no server. Your wallet stays on your device.

Last updated October 13, 2026

In plain terms

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • Your cards, spend logs and settings are stored only on your device. TapRightHere has no server to send them to.
  • To find the business you're at, the app asks Apple Maps about places near you. That request is made by your device to Apple. We don't receive it, store it or see it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

What TapRightHere is

TapRightHere is an iPhone app that tells you which credit card in your wallet earns the most at the place you're standing. It works without an account or any backend database.

What is stored, and where

The cards you add, the bonus categories you pick, spend you log against a cap or sign-up bonus, and your own point values are saved in one file in the app's private storage on your device (shared only with TapRightHere's own Home Screen widget and Control Center control through an Apple App Group). It is never uploaded anywhere.

Location

When you tap the location button, ask Siri, or use the Control Center control, TapRightHere asks iOS for your current location once and uses Apple's MapKit to look up nearby businesses. Your device sends that request to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. TapRightHere does not store your location history and has no server that could receive it.

Location access is While Using the App only. The app never asks for background or "Always" location.

Reports you choose to send

If a place is categorized wrongly, you can correct it on your device. You may also tap Share a Report to send a short text note through your own share sheet (Mail, Messages or another app you pick). TapRightHere never sees or transmits that message itself.

Third parties

The app uses Apple's CoreLocation and MapKit frameworks. It includes no advertising network, analytics library or crash-reporting SDK. If you install a test version through TestFlight, Apple's TestFlight service separately collects crash logs and any feedback you submit through it, under Apple's own terms.

Card and rewards data

The catalog of card names, reward rates and category rules bundled with the app is public reference information. It does not identify which cards you hold. That lives only in your wallet file on your device.

Children

TapRightHere is a general-audience finance utility and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example by adding household sharing through iCloud, this page will be updated before the change ships and will say exactly what leaves your device and why.
